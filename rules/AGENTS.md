Do not load `product/WORKFLOW.md`, `skills/xy-workflow/SKILL.md` or its business adapter, create a task graph, delegate, or apply business approval gates on this branch.

Sufficient conversation context requires no automatic Skill, project or plugin reads, initialization, graph, file writes or dispatch. For an explicitly invoked interview Skill, follow the shared workflow entry's Skill-selection rule before applying it. If specific facts require investigation, stay within the user's scope. Neither action authorizes planning or production. Never bypass a denied read by switching tools.
